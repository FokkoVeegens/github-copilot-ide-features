Privacy

How this site measures visits, and how to opt out.

← Back to the feature search

In short

This site counts visits with Umami Cloud, a privacy-focused analytics service. It does not use cookies, does not show ads, and does not build profiles of you. The statistics only help to see how much the site is used and which parts are useful.

What is collected

When you open the search page, your browser sends Umami:

  • the page address and title, and the page you came from (referrer);
  • your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, and language;
  • an approximate country, region, and city, derived from your IP address.

Umami does not store your IP address as a field in its standard analytics records. It processes your IP address and browser User-Agent to create a pseudonymous session identifier used for visitor metrics. The salt used to create that identifier changes monthly, so identifiers are not automatically linked across those periods. This does not guarantee anonymity: Umami or its infrastructure providers may also process or record IP addresses in operational or security logs. When you pause after entering a valid search (at least three characters), the normalized search term, the number of matching results, and whether launch-only filtering is enabled are sent as a custom Umami event. Search terms are lowercased and repeated whitespace is collapsed. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information in the search box.

The full list is in Umami's metric definitions.

Who processes it, and for how long

Umami Software, Inc. processes the statistics on behalf of this site under its data processing agreement. Umami may use third-party subprocessors to operate the service. On Umami's Hobby plan, analytics data is retained for six months; backup copies may be retained longer. See Umami's privacy policy and subprocessors for details.

The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as your IP address when serving pages. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Opt out

You can turn off visit counting for this browser. The choice is remembered in your browser's local storage on this site; clearing your site data resets it. Content blockers that block cloud.umami.is also prevent counting.

Enable JavaScript to change this setting, or use a content blocker.

Questions

This site does not send your name or account identifier to Umami. Its default analytics use pseudonymous identifiers, which are not a guarantee that activity cannot be linked to you. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
